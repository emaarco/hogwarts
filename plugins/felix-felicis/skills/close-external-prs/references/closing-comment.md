Thank you for taking the time to contribute. We really appreciate it.

We're currently not accepting external pull requests. As an organisation, we don't have the resources right now to fully review and validate contributions from outside the team, and most of them are AI-generated. Under these conditions we can't guarantee that a merged change keeps the product:

- free of bugs,
- free of security vulnerabilities or malicious code, and
- consistent with requirements that actually make sense for the product.

So for the time being we're closing externally created pull requests on most of our repositories, including this one.

If you've found a bug or have an idea, please open an issue instead. We read every one, and it's the best way to get something onto our radar.

Thanks for your understanding.
